## What "verified" means here
- **A**: confirmed by the company's own site or press release, or by trade press (Plastics News, Argus, ICIS, S&P).
- **B**: confirmed by two or more independent directories or customs-record aggregators (Panjiva, ImportGenius, Volza, Thomasnet).
- **C**: one source only, or self-reported. Check the company's registration and ask for trade references before paying.

All checks went through search-indexed sources. Company websites could not be opened directly from this environment. A rating means the company exists and publicly describes this business. It is **not** a credit or KYC check. Phone numbers appear only where a source listed one, so confirm them before calling.

## Indian rules that change the deal (check the current text with your customs broker)
- BIS quality-control orders for PE, PP, PVC, ABS, PC and EVA were **withdrawn on 12 Nov 2025**.
- BCD on PE/PP: 7.5% standard, plus 10% SWS on BCD, plus 18% IGST. BCD was nil from 2 Apr to 30 Jun 2026; confirm the rate in force today.
- S-PVC: anti-dumping duty was reportedly not imposed (Nov 2025). A **minimum import price of $0.766/kg CIF** applies for 6 months from 24 Jul 2026 (DGFT 25/2026-27; amended 30 Sep 2026).
- UAE origin gets CEPA concessions with tariff-rate quotas. ASEAN origin (Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam) gets AIFTA preference. Both need a valid certificate of origin.
- Import only **virgin material in primary form** (HS 3901/3902/3904). Regrind or scrap (HS 3915) sold as "off-grade" is effectively barred.
